FAQ

Questions

Is Kosh free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no account.

Which banks does it understand?

ICICI, HDFC, SBI, Axis, Kotak and DBS are read in detail; PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union Bank, IDFC FIRST, IndusInd, Yes Bank, AU and Federal Bank are recognised too, and a general parser covers the rest. For history SMS missed, import your bank statement as CSV. Anything it misreads lands in a Review inbox where you fix it once and Kosh learns.

Why does it need SMS permission?

To read transaction messages from your bank and turn them into entries automatically. Messages are processed on the phone and never uploaded anywhere.

Why does Google Play Protect warn me?

Android shows extra warnings for any app installed outside the Play Store that asks for SMS access, because scam apps abuse it. Kosh only reads messages to track your money, offline.

Will I lose my data when I update?

No. New versions install over the old one and keep everything. Turn on automatic backups in More → Automatic backups, or make one any time from More → Back up.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — iOS doesn't let apps read SMS, so Kosh is Android-only for now.
